# Collaboration

## Inviting people

Click **Share** at the top right of the editor:

- Projects are shared only with people who have an account on this server. Type part of a name or email, pick the account from the list, choose a role and click **+**.
- People without an account first need an invitation link from an admin or the system admin; see [Administration](#/help/admin).
- Change roles, transfer ownership or remove people in the member list. Only the owner manages members.

### Sharing with a group

The owner can share a project with a group they are in: in **Share**, type part of the group's name, pick it (groups are listed before people), choose a role and click **+**.

- Everyone who has joined the group gets that role. People who join the group later get it too; people who leave lose it.
- The group is listed with its number of members and its role, which is changed there; its ⋯ menu has **Stop sharing**.
- Its members show in the member list as "through" the group. Their role comes from the group; to give one of them another role, add them by hand, which takes them out of the group's hands.

### Roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including members and deleting the project; administrators can also publish it as a template |
| Editor | Edit and manage files, accept or reject suggestions |
| Reviewer | Change text only as tracked suggestions; comment |
| Viewer | Read, compile, download, comment |

## Editing together

Several people can edit the same file at once; each cursor has its own colour and the header shows who is here. Changes sync live; there is nothing to save.

## Comments

Select text and click the comment button in the toolbar (or press `Mod+Alt+M`). Comments are in the **Review** panel on the left, where you can reply, edit and resolve them. Type `@` to mention someone; they get a notification.

## Track changes

In the Review panel, turn track changes on for everyone or for some people. Edits then become suggestions:

- deleted text is struck through and new text highlighted;
- editors **accept** or **reject** them, one by one or all at once; a decision made by mistake can be taken back with **Undo** on the message that follows (for 15 minutes);
- reviewers' edits are always tracked; they can **withdraw** their own suggestions in the panel;
- your own mistakes: `Ctrl/Cmd+Z` undoes your tracked edits, and typing back text you deleted, where it was, restores it instead of adding a new suggestion;
- the PDF shows the text with the suggestions in it.

## Chat

**Chat** in the left rail is the project's chat:

- `Enter` sends, `Shift+Enter` starts a new line.
- `@` mentions a member.
- Maths in `$…$` is typeset, e.g. `$x^2$`.

## Notifications

Mentions in chat or comments, replies to your comments, being added to a project and invitations to groups show up in **Notifications**, with an unread count in the sidebar. They are in ColTex only; no email.
