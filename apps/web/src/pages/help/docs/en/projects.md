# Projects

## The project list

- **Workspace**: every project you own or are a member of; search, switch between list and cards, filter by owner.
- **Shared with me**: projects other people invited you to.

A project's menu (⋯) has: open, make a copy, download .zip, download PDF, convert to template, publish as template (owners), archive and move to trash. Select several projects in the list to act on them together.

## Tags

**Tags** in the sidebar sort your projects; click one to see only its projects. Tags are yours alone and never change what other members see. Click a project's tags (the Tags column in the list, or on its card; with none, **+ Tag** shows on hover) to tick tags or make a new one, or select several in the list.

## Archive and trash

- **Archive** takes finished projects out of your list without changing them; unarchive them any time.
- Projects in the **Trash** can be restored. From the trash, the owner can **Delete forever** (for everyone); other members **Leave project**.

> Archive and trash change only your own list, until the owner deletes a project for good.

## Copies

**Make a copy** creates a new project of yours with all files and settings, but not the collaborators, history or comments. Viewers can make copies too.
