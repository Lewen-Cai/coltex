# Settings

Click your avatar at the bottom left and choose **Settings**.

- **Account**: your display name (shown to collaborators, in comments and in the history) and password. Changing the password can sign out your other devices. Passwords need 8 or more characters with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a number; symbols are optional. The rating shown while typing (weak, fair, good, strong) rises with length and symbols. Sign-up and admin resets follow the same rules. Your sign-in email can only be changed by an administrator.
- **Appearance**: light, dark or system theme, and the interface language (English / 简体中文). Dark mode applies to the editor too; PDF pages stay white. Kept in this browser.
- **Sync services**: connect your Zotero account (see [References](#/help/references)), and create and revoke git tokens (see [Files](#/help/files)): each one for Git, MCP or both, read and write or read only, for all your projects or some, with an expiry. Use one token per computer, so you can revoke it on its own.
- **AI agent**: your AI usage over the last 7, 30 or 90 days (requests, tokens, cost, per day and per model) and what is left of your limit (when you have the AI agent), what AI agents may change for you (**Permissions**), and connecting your own AI agents over **MCP**, with their tokens; see [AI agent](#/help/ai).
- **AI skills** (when you have the AI agent): your own skills, the built-in ones, and a chat with the AI agent for writing skills; see [AI agent](#/help/ai).

Editor settings (font size, colours, Vim…) are under **Settings** at the bottom of the editor's left rail; see [Editor](#/help/editor).
