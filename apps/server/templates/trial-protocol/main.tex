\begin{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Introduction:} State the problem and why the trial is needed.\\
\textbf{Methods and analysis:} Describe the design, participants, interventions, primary outcome and sample size.\\
\textbf{Ethics and dissemination:} Say who approved the protocol and how the results will be shared.\\
\textbf{Trial registration:} Registry and number, e.g. ClinicalTrials.gov NCT00000000.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} protocol; randomised controlled trial; primary care

\section{Introduction}
Summarise the evidence and the question the trial answers. The protocol follows
SPIRIT.\cite{chan2013spirit}

\section{Methods and analysis}
\subsection{Trial design and setting}
Describe the design, the allocation ratio and where the trial takes place.

\subsection{Eligibility criteria}
List the inclusion and exclusion criteria.

\subsection{Interventions}
Describe each arm in enough detail to replicate it.

\subsection{Outcomes}
Define the primary and secondary outcomes and when they are measured.

\subsection{Sample size}
Justify the number of participants. For a difference $\delta$ in means with
standard deviation $\sigma$,
\begin{equation}
  n = \frac{2 (z_{1-\alpha/2} + z_{1-\beta})^2 \sigma^2}{\delta^2}
\end{equation}
participants per arm are needed.

\subsection{Randomisation and blinding}
Describe the sequence generation, allocation concealment and who is blinded.

\subsection{Statistical analysis}
Describe the primary analysis and how missing data are handled.

\subsection{Data monitoring}
Describe the data monitoring committee and the stopping rules.

\section{Ethics and dissemination}
Name the ethics committee and say how participants give consent, following the
Declaration of Helsinki.\cite{wma2013helsinki}

\section*{Declarations}
\noindent\textbf{Ethics approval:} Name the committee and the approval number.\\
\textbf{Funding:} Name the funders and their role.\\
\textbf{Competing interests:} Declare any, or state that there are none.\\
\textbf{Data availability:} Say where the data can be found.\\
\textbf{Author contributions:} Say who did what.
