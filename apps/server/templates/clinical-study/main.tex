\subsection{Statistical analysis}
Describe the methods used to compare groups. The primary outcome was analysed
with a linear model adjusted for the baseline value,
\begin{equation}
  y_i = \beta_0 + \beta_1\,\mathrm{treatment}_i + \beta_2\,\mathrm{baseline}_i + \varepsilon_i ,
\end{equation}
and $\beta_1$ is reported with its 95\% confidence interval.
